Position Summary

Stock positions only (options excluded — no reliable daily price series here), built by FIFO-matching purchases against sales/exchanges. P/L (Running) applies to still-open positions (vs. today's price); % P/L (Annualized) applies to closed lots (CAGR-style, using the estimated buy/sell prices). Every number here sits two layers of estimation below the actual disclosed filing — treat as directional, not exact. Short holding periods can produce extreme-looking annualized figures (a small loss over a few days can annualize to nearly −100%) — check "Held" and the underlying $ amount before reading too much into the annualized percentage alone.
